Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This is a template Privacy Policy for Aesthetic Lifestyle Touch. It hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer — please have it checked before relying on it for a real, live store.

1. What This Covers

This policy explains what information Aesthetic Lifestyle Touch collects when you use this site, and how it's used and stored.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide: name, email, phone number, password.
  • Order details: shipping/billing address, items purchased, payment method, and — for mobile wallet payments — the wallet number and Transaction ID you submit.
  • Activity on the site: cart contents, wishlist, order history, notifications, and support messages.

3. How We Store It

Right now, this site runs entirely in your browser — there is no remote server or database. Everything above is saved in your browser's local storage on the device you're using. It stays on that device and browser; it does not sync to your other devices, and we (the store) don't have a separate, central copy of it. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

4. How We Use Your Information

To process and track your orders, manage your account, respond to support requests, verify mobile wallet payments, and show you relevant information like order status and notifications. We don't sell your information to anyone.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage (not traditional cookies) to keep you signed in and remember your cart, wishlist, and preferences between visits.

6. Third-Party Services

We load fonts from Google Fonts and, in some places, demo product imagery from Unsplash. These third parties may receive standard technical request information (like your IP address) when those assets load, under their own privacy practices.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Since your data lives in your own browser, you're already in control of it — you can review, export, or delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain, or by asking us via the Contact page if you'd like help doing so.

8. Children's Privacy

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves — for example, if we move to a real server-based backend in the future, this page will be updated to reflect that.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Contact page.
