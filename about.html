Our Story

It started with a drawer full of things we never used again.

Somewhere between the desk gadgets that broke in a month and the decor that looked cheap in daylight, we started asking a different question: what if a store only sold things worth keeping?

That meant saying no to almost everything. Most products that cross our desk don't make it — not because they're bad, but because they're forgettable. What's left is a small, considered collection of objects we'd genuinely want in our own homes.

We're still small. We still personally check the things we sell. That's not a marketing line — it's just how we've kept the standard from slipping as we've grown.

"We'd rather carry one honest product than ten impressive ones."
